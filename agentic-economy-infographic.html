The Agentic Economy — Corpus Analysis

51 definitions · 6 categories · 8 coded dimensions · 3 visualizations

Settlement Fault Lines

Category Ccr is 100% crypto-native; Category A is 100% fiat-adjacent (12 of 14 entries coded fiat, 1 hybrid). Category B and E leave settlement unstated — infrastructure and speculative framings deliberately avoid the question. Only 3 of 51 entries span the hybrid middle ground.

When the Field Was Named

The field has a founding moment and a naming moment. The founding: 3 Ccr entries in 2018-2021 (crypto-native definitions predating mainstream attention). The naming: 44 of 51 definitions were published in 2025-2026, with the densest cluster in Q4 2025 through Q1 2026. Tick height encodes cross-references — the tallest ticks (C7, A1, C8) are the most-cited anchors.

The Ideological Axis

Each dot is one of 51 definitions. Categories cluster along the diagonal: Category A (blue) sits at present/human-in-loop; Category Ccr (amber) and the speculative entries occupy near-future and speculative/autonomous. 31 of 51 entries (61%) code actor_model as autonomous — the field has already decided.
