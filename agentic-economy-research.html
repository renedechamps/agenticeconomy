Two Economies, Not One - A Taxonomy of the Agentic Economy and the Case for Settlement Neutrality

zenodo.org/records/19208278

Maps 51 definitions from 40+ sources across 6 categories. Reveals a fundamental fault line between human-mediated (Category A) and machine-autonomous (Category C) visions. Introduces the category system used throughout this site.

Key Findings

  • 51 definitions from industry, academia, and standards bodies
  • 6 categories spanning commerce, workforce, blockchain, regulation, and infrastructure
  • The 11:1 ratio: 11 definitions describe agent-to-agent crypto commerce vs. 1 proposing traditional settlement

Published 2026

Commerce Readiness Index for Agentic Economy Protocols

10.5281/zenodo.19208083

Proposes a quantitative framework to evaluate how ready protocols are for real agent commerce. Scores 17 protocols across dimensions like settlement finality, identity, dispute resolution, and openness. Reveals that no single protocol is fully commerce-ready.

Key Findings

  • 17 protocols scored across 8 dimensions
  • Payment-first protocols (Stripe, Visa) score high on settlement but low on agent autonomy
  • Crypto protocols (x402, Olas) score high on autonomy but low on dispute resolution

03
Published 2026

The Oracle Problem in Agent-to-Agent Commerce

zenodo.org/records/19208440

Examines the fundamental trust problem when AI agents transact without human oversight. How does an agent verify that another agent delivered what was promised? Proposes Settlement Neutrality as a framework with 5 formal properties.

Key Findings

  • Defines the "oracle problem" for agent commerce
  • Settlement Neutrality: 5 properties (Escrow Determinism, Ledger Consistency, Reputation Equivalence, Dispute Parity, Auditability)
  • Neither blockchain nor traditional rails alone solve the problem
