About the Field Reports series

Field Reports are tactical, dated, regulatory-focused documents — the second editorial series alongside the AgenticEconomy.dev research preprints. Preprints are formal research; Field Reports are operating manuals. Each carries a series ID, a publication date, a clear declaration of independence, and a Zenodo DOI for permanent citation.

All Field Reports are independent policy briefs. None are official publications of the European Union or any other public institution.
