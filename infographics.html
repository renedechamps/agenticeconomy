Agentic Economy Research Series / Infographics

Research, visualized.

One infographic per preprint — each distilling the paper's core argument into a single canvas. Released under CC-BY 4.0; feel free to share, remix, or reprint with attribution.

Series overview

The infographics are designed to stand alone. Each one can be screenshotted, shared in a slack, or dropped into a slide deck without needing to have read the corresponding paper — but they also serve as visual anchors for readers who come back to the papers later. Click any image to open it at full resolution (2752 × 1536 px).

Anatomy of Agent Trust
